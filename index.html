Privacy Policy - Calcul Express

Effective date: November 8, 2026

1. About Calcul Express

Calcul Express is a single-player arithmetic puzzle game.

2. Information We Collect

The current version does not require an account or registration. It does not use analytics, crash-reporting services, or online leaderboards. The developer does not collect or receive personal information through the game.

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer's servers. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app may erase this information.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The current version does not include third-party advertising SDKs. If advertising or other third-party services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated to explain their data practices.

5. Children's Privacy

The current version does not knowingly collect personal information from users.

6. Data Security

Game data is stored locally on your device. Please protect access to your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change.
